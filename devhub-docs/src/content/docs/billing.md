DevHub sells **per-workspace** plans. Prices, durations, and limits are always shown before you pay. The in-app `/pricing` page shows the same options described here.

## Plans

| Plan | Members | Projects | Billing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 2 | 3 | Free forever |
| Paid packages (e.g. Pro) | Generous or unlimited, per package | Generous or unlimited, per package | Per duration shown at checkout |

A retired package keeps working until its expiry date but cannot be purchased again.

## Paying (Pakasir QRIS / virtual-account)

1. Pick a workspace → pick a package → pick a duration → **Pay via Pakasir**.
2. Checkout creates a **pending** order and opens the Pakasir payment page (`app.pakasir.com`), where you complete the QRIS/virtual-account payment.
3. Every payment is confirmed directly with Pakasir before your plan activates. A duplicate notification never activates twice.
4. We store only the order ID, amount, and status. **VA numbers and QR codes are never stored** — they live only on the Pakasir page.

If the smaller package you picked can't fit your current usage, checkout tells you and refuses the downgrade until usage fits.

## Renewals & grace period

- **Manual renewal** — no auto-charge. Renewals **stack**: paying early extends from your current expiry date, so you never lose paid days.
- **Upgrades and same-package renewals apply instantly.** Moving to a smaller package takes effect when the current period ends.
- **7-day grace:** after expiry the workspace returns to Free limits, but everything stays **read-only** for 7 days — nothing is deleted. Renew within that window to continue seamlessly.
- Cancel a **pending** order any time from the Billing page.

## Refunds

**No refunds after activation**, except (a) duplicate charges for the same workspace/period, or (b) a payment that never activated within **24 hours** despite a completed charge. Claim via **support@devhub.nrawangbatin.my.id** with your order ID; verified duplicates/failures are refunded or credited. Chargeback abuse may lead to suspension.

## FAQ

**Where is my QR / VA number?** On the Pakasir payment page only — we never store or re-show it. While the order is pending, use *Resume payment* on the Billing page to get the link again.

**My payment completed but the plan is not active?** Activation follows automatic confirmation — give it a moment, then refresh the Billing page. If 24 hours pass with a completed charge and no activation, email support with your order ID.

**Can I get an invoice?** Your order ID, amount, and date in Team billing / Payment history is the record. Need more? Email support.
