## 5. Plans, Billing & Refunds (Pakasir)

- **Plans:** Free (2 members / 3 projects) and paid workspace packages (e.g. Pro with unlimited members & projects). Limits, durations, and prices are displayed before checkout.
- **Payments** are processed by **Pakasir** (QRIS/virtual-account). Checkout creates a pending order and redirects you to the Pakasir payment page. We store only the order ID, amount, and status plus a verification log; **virtual-account numbers and QR payloads are never stored**.
- **Activation:** every payment is confirmed directly with Pakasir before your plan activates. Failed confirmations never activate a plan, and a duplicate notification never activates twice.
- **Renewal is manual** — there is no auto-charge. Each successful payment extends the expiry from the later of the old expiry or the payment time, so paying early never wastes days.
- **Cancel a pending order** any time from the Billing page (payer or team admin). Completed payments cannot be "cancelled" — see refunds.
- **Refunds:** **no refunds after activation**, except (a) duplicate charges for the same workspace/period, or (b) a payment that never activated within **24 hours** despite a completed charge. Claims go to **support@devhub.nrawangbatin.my.id** with your order ID; verified duplicates/failures are refunded or credited. Chargeback abuse may lead to suspension.
- **Expiry & grace:** on expiry the workspace reverts to Free limits. For **7 days after expiry** over-quota content remains **read-only** (existing data stays visible; adding projects/members beyond the Free quota is paused). Renew any time without losing paid days. Moving to a smaller package takes effect at expiry; upgrades and same-package renewals apply instantly. A retired package keeps working until its expiry date but cannot be repurchased.
- **Disputes about charges:** contact support first; we respond within 3×24 hours on business days.
